      About your data

      Your microphone sends audio to ElevenLabs while the assistant is active. When it needs to look, a camera frame and your goal go through our server to Google Gemini. Nearby search shares location with Google only when you ask to find a place and grant permission.

      This app keeps goals and conversation context in this tab’s memory and does not save images, audio, or transcripts on its server. Pause keeps that context; “Forget this session” clears it here. Provider retention follows your service configuration and their policies.

      Camera guidance is for framing objects and reading information. It is not a mobility or hazard-detection aid.
