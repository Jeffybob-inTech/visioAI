        About your data.

        Your microphone sends audio to ElevenLabs while the assistant is active. When it needs to look, a camera frame and your goal go through our server to Google Gemini. Web lookups send your search query to Google. Nearby search shares location with Google only when you ask to find a place and grant permission.

        This app keeps goals and conversation context in this tab’s memory and does not save images, audio, or transcripts on its server. Pause keeps that context; “Forget this session” clears it here. Provider retention follows your service configuration and their policies.
